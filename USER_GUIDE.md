# 🩺 Nurse Learning Corner - User Guide

Welcome to your specialized nursing curriculum portal. This guide will help you master the app and excel in your studies.

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. **Login/Register**: Use your institutional email to create an account.
2. **Setup Profile**: Choose your **Program** (e.g., Registered Nursing) and **Year of Study** to filter content specifically for you.

## 📚 Study Features
### 1. The Digital Library
- **Browse**: Find documents, Past Papers, and Marking Keys.
- **Search**: Use the top search bar to find specific topics or subjects.
- **Filter**: Tap the filter chips (e.g., "Past Paper") to quickly sort resources.

### 2. Immersive Study Viewer
- **PDF Navigation**: Swipe UP/DOWN or LEFT/RIGHT to turn pages.
- **Smart Resume**: The app remembers exactly where you left off. When you return to a document, you'll be asked if you want to continue from your last page.
- **System Fallback**: If a complex document struggles to load, use the **"Open in System Viewer"** button at the bottom to open it in your phone's native browser or PDF app.

### 3. Study Notebook
- While reading any document, tap the **Blue Notebook Icon** (bottom right).
- Capture your thoughts, mnemonics, or key points.
- Your notes are **Cloud Synced**—they follow you if you switch devices!
- Access all reflections via **Account > Study Notebook**.

### 4. Memory Decks (Flashcards)
- Use flashcards to master difficult concepts.
- Simply tap a card to flip it and reveal the answer.
- Your progress in each deck is saved automatically.

## 📶 Offline Access
- Tap the **Cloud Download Icon** at the top of any resource to save it.
- Downloaded items can be accessed without internet from **Library > Downloads**.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Stability
- **Visible Recents**: You can now see your study progress in your phone's "Minimized Apps" view for quick switching.
- **Device Locking**: Your account is securely locked to your primary device to protect your study profile.

## 🆘 Support
Need help? Go to **Account > Nurse Support Desk** to:
- Chat instantly with us on **WhatsApp**.
- Send a detailed **Email** for academic inquiries.
